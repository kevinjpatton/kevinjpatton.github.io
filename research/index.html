Research & Publications

My research program primarily investigates the friction points between traditional moral frameworks and emerging digital technologies. I am particularly interested in how we assign moral agency in an age of automated systems.

Peer-Reviewed Publications

Forthcoming, 2027

The Moral Weight of the Algorithm

Patton, K. Journal of Applied Philosophy.

This paper argues that current models for understanding automated decision-making fail to adequately capture the diffused moral responsibility across development teams.

Read Pre-print
2025

Epistemic Uncertainty in Neural Networks

Patton, K. & Smith, J. Philosophy & Technology, 38(4), 45-62.

An exploration of the "black box" problem in AI from a strictly epistemological standpoint, questioning what it means to "know" a result generated by a machine.
